The Idea · Live

Tell it where you are. Get your path.

Voice intake → personalized path → “explain this simply” → share with a caseworker. Multilingual, low-literacy friendly, calm by design.

02
The Idea · Dignity by design

It cannot lie to you.

  • Never invents a shelter or hotlineOnly real, universal U.S. systems — 211, HUD, Vital Records, SSA, 988
  • Your story lives on your deviceNothing on a server. We can't leak what we never hold.
