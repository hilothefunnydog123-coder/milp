# YNorth — Pitch

### "Find your way home."

> **YNorth turns the invisible maze of getting out of homelessness — waitlists, forms,
> offices that don't talk to each other — into a clear, plain-language, step-by-step
> path you own.** Tell it where you are (voice or text, multilingual, low-literacy
> friendly) and get a personalized ordered map (Now / Soon / The path home), each step
> with one concrete action, the documents you'll need, and a **real, verified** resource.
> Privacy by design: your story lives on *your device*, nothing on a server. The AI
> **can never invent a fake shelter** — it may only cite real universal U.S. systems
> (211, Coordinated Entry, Vital Records, SSA, HUD vouchers, 988). And a tiny on-device
> learning "brain" makes the whole thing smarter every time someone uses it.

Built for the **Housing Dignity** track at Milpitas Hacks.

## 🎤 The full spoken pitch (narration for the run of show above)

**[Open quiet. One beat.]**

"Picture losing your home tomorrow. Not your money — your *home*. Where do you go? You'd
think the hard part is finding help. It's not. The help *exists* — there are vouchers,
shelters, benefits, legal aid, all of it.

The hard part is that it's an **invisible maze.** Twelve different waitlists. Forms that
need an ID you lost. A Coordinated Entry system nobody explained to you. Offices that
don't talk to each other. People don't stay homeless because help doesn't exist — **they
stay homeless because the path to it is hidden.**

**[Beat.]** This is YNorth. A north star out of that maze.

You tell it where you are — in your own words, by voice, in any language, even when words
are hard. And it gives you back something nobody's ever handed you: **your path.** An
ordered map — what to do *right now*, what's next, and the road to a stable home. Every
step in plain language, with the one document you need and a *real* phone number that
actually works.

**[The trust line — slow down here]** And here's what makes YNorth different from every
chatbot you've seen. **It cannot lie to you.** It will never invent a shelter, never make
up a hotline. It only points you to real, universal systems — 211, HUD, Vital Records,
988. And your story? It never touches our servers. It lives on *your* device. **Because
dignity isn't a feature. It's the whole point.**

**[The kicker]** And every time someone uses YNorth and says 'this helped,' it gets a
little smarter — so the next mother fleeing with two kids gets the path that actually
worked for the last one. **It's collective intelligence for getting housed.**

**[Close]** We can't fix the housing crisis this weekend. But we can make sure that no
one — no one — stays lost in the maze just because they couldn't find the door. **YNorth
points home.**"

## 🛡️ Tough-question prep

- **"How is this different from just asking ChatGPT?"** → ChatGPT will confidently invent
  a shelter that doesn't exist and a phone number that rings nowhere — *catastrophic* for
  someone in crisis. YNorth is hard-constrained to a curated list of real universal U.S.
  systems. Trustworthy by construction, not by hope.
- **"What about privacy — these are vulnerable people?"** → That's the core design choice.
  Nothing about a user touches a server; the path lives on their device and in links
  *they* choose to generate. We can't leak what we never hold.
- **"Does it actually help if it can't book the shelter?"** → The #1 barrier isn't
  booking — it's *knowing the ordered next step and the document you need for it.* We
  remove the navigation barrier, which is what keeps people stuck. Sharing the path with a
  caseworker closes the loop.
- **"How does it reach people without phones/internet?"** → Distributed *through* the
  institutions people already touch — libraries, shelters, 211, social workers — who run
  it with/for the person. Voice + low-literacy design is for exactly this population.
- **"Is this sustainable / a business?"** → Mission-first: free to the person in need,
  offered as civic infrastructure to cities and 211 networks (the same way they fund
  existing navigation services). The learning brain compounds its value over time.
- **"What did you build this weekend?"** → A working product: multilingual voice + text
  intake, Gemini-generated personalized path with plain-language "explain this simply" on
  every step, document checklists, a real-resource-only safety layer, an advocate share
  view with no server, and an on-device learning model — plus a fallback so it works even
  with no API key.
